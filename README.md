## Fuad Mammadov

I work on eye tracking and computer vision for assistive technology and attention-aware systems. MSc student in Interactive Media Technology at KTH in Stockholm, Swedish Institute scholar. Before that I spent four years at a creative technology lab in Baku leading XR and robotics projects that shipped to real users.

Mechatronics engineer by training. I like building the whole loop, from the camera to the model to the thing that moves or lights up on the desk.

### Now

Training a CNN gaze estimation baseline on MPIIGaze and evaluating it person-independently, in [GazeMap](https://github.com/fammad/GazeMap). Autumn courses are visual data analysis and image analysis at KTH. Looking for a master's thesis in eye tracking or computer vision for 2027.

### Projects

| | |
|---|---|
| [GazeMap](https://github.com/fammad/GazeMap) | Appearance-based gaze estimation on MPIIGaze with leave-one-person-out evaluation. Includes a worked example of how a random split leaks near-duplicate frames and inflates the result. PyTorch, CPU-only. |
| [Lumen](https://github.com/fammad/Lumen_blink_detection) | Webcam blink detection (MediaPipe eye aspect ratio) with a rolling personal baseline, driving an ESP32-S3 desk light over serial. F1 0.92 under head turns, validated across six lighting and distance conditions. |
| [EmotionRadar](https://github.com/fammad/EmotionRadar) | Multi-label emotion classification on 58k GoEmotions comments. Class-balanced logistic regression over TF-IDF, macro-F1 0.51, with a documented negative clustering result. Reproducible in Colab. |
| [Qonur](https://github.com/fammad/Qonur_Robotic_Bear) | Conversational animatronic bear for children. Ten servos, ESP32 firmware, Python controller, 3D-printable body. Deployed in a children's shelter and a rural village in Azerbaijan. |
